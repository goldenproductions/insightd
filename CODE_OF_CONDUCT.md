# Code of Conduct

This project follows a simple rule: **be kind, stay on-topic, assume good faith.**

We're a small open-source community building a homelab tool. The space is welcoming to people of any background, experience level, or stack preference. Treat fellow contributors and users the way you'd want to be treated when you're new to a codebase.

## What we expect

- Polite, constructive communication in issues, pull requests, and discussions.
- Disagreements about code or design framed around the work, not the person.
- Patience with maintainers and contributors who reply on their own schedule — this is a hobby project for most people involved.
- English as the working language for issues and PRs (so others can find and learn from them later).

## What's not okay

- Personal attacks, harassment, or sustained disruption.
- Posting other people's private information without consent.
- Off-topic political or ideological campaigning in project channels.
- Repeated low-effort or AI-generated noise on issues and PRs.

Maintainers will edit, lock, or remove content that violates these expectations, and may restrict participation in extreme cases.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behavior that needs maintainer attention:

- For most concerns, mention `@goldenproductions` in the relevant thread.
- For sensitive matters (harassment, threats, anything you'd rather not post publicly), use [GitHub's private vulnerability reporting](../../security) — same channel as security reports — and label your message "Code of Conduct".

Reports will be reviewed by the maintainer in confidence. Outcomes range from a private conversation to permanent ban depending on severity.
